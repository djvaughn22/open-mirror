# Etsy Listing Images — Old Laptop to Build Machine

Internal spec for review. Etsy allows up to 10 images per listing. Specs
below; produce them from the guide's design system (deep blue `#123049`,
teal `#0b6a80`, warm-white background, Georgia + Segoe UI). **Do not publish.**

## Format basics

- Etsy displays listing images at a **square** thumbnail; the primary image
  should read well as a square. Upload at **2000 × 2000 px** (square) or
  **2000 × 1600 px** (4:5) minimum, sRGB, JPG or PNG.
- Keep important text within the center safe area — the thumbnail crops edges.
- Also export **vertical 1080 × 1350 (4:5)** and **square 1080 × 1080** social
  crops of images 1, 3, and 10 for Instagram/Pinterest reuse.

## Thumbnail hierarchy

1. Image 1 (hero) must win the grid — largest title, highest contrast, clear
   even at 170 px. Test it shrunk.
2. Images 2, 3, 6 are the strongest supporting sells (payoff, contents, the
   real finished site) — keep them early.
3. Images 5–9 build trust and answer objections.
4. Image 10 (free check) last — a soft, generous closer.

## Mockup guidance

- Show the product as tangible: render the guide PDF as a **device mockup**
  (laptop screen and/or tablet) and a **printed-and-bound** look for the
  worksheet/checklist, so buyers grasp "what do I actually get."
- Use one consistent template (same fonts, colors, margins) across all ten so
  the set reads as one product.
- Keep photos of laptops generic/older to match the audience — no branding.
- Never fabricate reviews, ratings, "bestseller," or customer counts in an
  image.
