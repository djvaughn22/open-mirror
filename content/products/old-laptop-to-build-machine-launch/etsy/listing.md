## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to code already?** No. This is written for complete
beginners; every term is explained.

**Will it work on my exact laptop?** It works on most laptops that power on and
have ~2 GB of RAM. The included free readiness check tells you before you buy
the process any time.

**Does it delete what's on my laptop?** Installing Linux erases the drive — so
you back up first (the guide shows how). Treat it as a laptop you're wiping.

**Is anything else needed — subscriptions, paid apps?** No. Every tool used is
free. No subscriptions, no paid APIs.

**What do I actually receive?** A digital download bundle: the guide (PDF/HTML/
Markdown), printable worksheets and checklists, reference cards, safe setup
scripts, and finished project files.

**Can I print it?** Yes — the guide, worksheet, and checklist are print-ready.
