# Launch materials — Old Laptop to Build Machine

**Internal only. This folder is NOT part of the customer bundle and is never
included in the downloadable ZIP.** It holds everything needed to take the
product to market, for DJ's review and approval. Nothing here is published
automatically; nothing here contains pricing or a checkout.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `launch-decisions.md` | Launch sequence, pricing options (for approval), free/paid boundaries, channel differences, support questions, versioning, and the go/no-go checklist. **Start here.** |
| `etsy/listing.md` | Etsy listing: title options, description, 13 tags, what's included, requirements, safety, digital-download explanation, FAQ, refund/support wording. |
| `etsy/listing-images.md` | Specs and exact text for the 10 listing images, social crops, thumbnail hierarchy, and mockup guidance. |
| `amazon-kdp/kdp-metadata.md` | Book title/subtitle options, description, faceless author approach, trim size, Kindle notes, cover brief, keyword/category research notes. |
| `amazon-kdp/manuscript.md` | The instructional content as a coherent, ordered book manuscript (front matter, chapters, safety disclaimer, back matter). Scripts are referred to companion downloads, not dumped inline. |
| `direct/product-page-copy.md` | Copy for selling directly on Open Mirror's own site: promise, who it's for / not for, included, requirements, safety, FAQ, support boundaries. |
| `social/content-package.md` | Instagram / YouTube Shorts / long-form YouTube: 10 short concepts, 3 long outlines, 10 hooks, 10 captions, before/after and required posts, CTA rotation. |

## Hard rules (apply to everything in this folder)

- Do not publish, open, or modify any live Etsy/Amazon/social listing from these
  drafts — they are copy to be reviewed and used deliberately.
- No public pricing until DJ approves it; no subscription model.
- No guaranteed results, no income or job promises.
- No fabricated testimonials, ratings, review counts, follower counts, or
  availability.
- Facelessness: the brand is Open Mirror; no owner name, photo, or bio anywhere.
