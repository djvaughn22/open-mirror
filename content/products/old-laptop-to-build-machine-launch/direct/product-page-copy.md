# Direct Product-Page Copy — Old Laptop to Build Machine

Internal draft for when Open Mirror sells this directly (its own site). **No
checkout system yet, no price here.** No guaranteed-results language.

## One-sentence promise

Turn an unused laptop into a simple Linux development machine — and publish your
first website.

## Who it's for

- People with an old or spare laptop they'd like to make useful again.
- Complete beginners — no experience with Linux, terminals, code, or Git needed.
- Anyone who learns best by doing, and wants to finish with a real, live result
  rather than just watching videos.
- Parents or mentors setting up a first "maker" machine for a curious teen.

## Who it's not for

- People whose laptop won't power on or is physically broken — this can't fix
  hardware.
- Anyone who needs a zero-effort, phone-style setup; this is hands-on and takes
  a few focused hours.
- Anyone hoping for a shortcut to a job or an income — this teaches a real
  skill, honestly, but promises no outcome beyond a working machine and a live
  site.
- People who need to keep their laptop exactly as it is (it gets wiped).

## Starting point

An old laptop, an internet connection, a USB stick to erase, and a couple of
free hours. That's it.

## Finished result

- A laptop running Linux, updated and secured.
- The tools developers use — a code editor, Git, Python, Node.js — installed and
  working.
- A small website you built by hand.
- That website live on the internet, at a real address you can share.
- A clear idea of what to build next.

## What's included

- The full illustrated guide (PDF), plus HTML and Markdown versions.
- Printable laptop-readiness worksheet and master checklist.
- Beginner terminal reference card and a Git & GitHub reference card.
- A troubleshooting & recovery guide.
- A safe, commented setup script and a read-only verification script.
- The complete finished first-project files.
- A start-here file, license, support notes, and version history.

## Requirements

- A laptop that powers on and boots reliably (its age matters less than you'd
  think).
- At least 2 GB of RAM (4 GB or more recommended) and ~20 GB of free storage.
- A USB flash drive, 8 GB or larger, you can erase.
- An internet connection.
- Supported path: Linux Mint 22 (Cinnamon, or Xfce for older hardware).

## Safety warning

Installing Linux erases everything on the laptop. The guide shows how to back up
first — please read that chapter before you begin. Treat this as a laptop you're
willing to wipe. Nothing in the bundle erases a drive automatically; the one
destructive step happens inside the Linux installer, under your control.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need any experience?** No — it's written for absolute beginners, and
every term is explained.

**Will it work on my laptop?** It works on most laptops that power on and have
about 2 GB of RAM or more. The free readiness check tells you before you commit.

**Does everything stay free to run?** Yes. Every tool used is free — no
subscriptions, no paid apps or APIs.

**What do I actually receive?** A digital download: the guide in three formats,
printable sheets, reference cards, safe scripts, and the finished project files.

**Can I print it?** Yes — the guide, worksheet, and checklist are print-ready.

## Support boundaries

Support covers questions about the product's own steps and files, at
ask@openmirrorllc.com. It does not include remote hands-on repair, fixing broken
hardware, obscure Wi-Fi drivers, other Linux distributions, or custom
deployments beyond the free static hosting the guide uses. The included
troubleshooting & recovery guide resolves the most common issues on its own.
