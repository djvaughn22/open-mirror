Laptop Readiness Worksheet

Answer these before installing anything. There's no wrong answer.

1. How old is the laptop, roughly?

  • Under 5 years
  • 5–10 years
  • Over 10 years

2. How much RAM does it have?

  • 8 GB or more
  • 4 GB
  • 2 GB or less

3. Hard drive (HDD) or solid-state drive (SSD)?

4. Does it power on and boot reliably?
