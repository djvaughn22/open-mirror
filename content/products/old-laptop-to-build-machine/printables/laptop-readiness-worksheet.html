Laptop Readiness Worksheet

Answer these before installing anything. There's no wrong answer — this tells you what to expect.

1. Does it power on and boot reliably?

  • Yes, starts up normally and holds a charge
  • Has an issue (note it):

2. Do the screen, keyboard, and trackpad work?

  • Yes, all working
  • Something doesn't (I can use an external one)

3. How much RAM (memory)?

  • 8 GB or more — comfortable
  • 4 GB — good with the Xfce edition
  • 2 GB — usable; choose Xfce

4. About how much free storage? (need ~20 GB)

5. Do I have a spare USB stick, 8 GB or larger, I can erase?

  • Yes
  • Need to get one

6. Is there anything on this laptop I need to keep?

  • Nothing important — ready to move on
  • Yes — I will back it up first (Chapter 2)

Which edition will I install?

  • Cinnamon (capable laptop, 8 GB+)
  • Xfce (older hardware / 4 GB or less)
