# Open Mirror Etsy — Organic Social Launch Plan

## Account Structure

**One primary account:** @OpenMirrorLLC (Instagram)

**Why:** Family identity matters more than individual brand silos. Products are extensions of Open Mirror's mission, not separate brands chasing followers.

**Why not separate accounts:** Takes 5x the content to maintain. Audience is too small to justify. Cross-family discovery is stronger than niche audiences right now.

## Posting Rhythm (Sustainable)

**3 posts/week** (square, 1200x1200px)
- Monday: Product feature or story
- Wednesday: Brand story or behind-the-scenes
- Friday: CTA or user story

**1 Reel/week** (vertical, 9:16)
- Hook in first 0.5 seconds
- Show the product or concept
- CTA at the end
- Use trending audio or clean cuts

**3 Stories/week** (when relevant)
- Polls ("Which one would you use?")
- Sneak peeks ("This drops Thursday")
- Announcements

**No paid ads.** Organic growth only.

## Content Pillars (Ratio)

- **Product launches** (60%): New products, features, social concepts
- **Brand stories** (20%): Why we made it, what it solves, mission
- **Community** (20%): User stories, testimonials, cross-promotion within Open Mirror family

## Copy Style (Non-Negotiable)

- **DJ's voice only.** Short, plain, human. Never marketing-speak.
- **Honest about what it is.** No fake hype. "A sticker pack for dog lovers" not "Transform your life with rescue dog art."
- **One clear action.** "Get it" or "Shop now." Not "Discover," "explore," "learn more."
- **No emojis unless they feel natural.** Less is more.

## Link Strategy

**Bio link:** openmirrorllc.com/shop (routes to product categories)

**Post links:** Every CTA links to either:
- Direct Etsy listing (once live)
- /shop (product category)
- Dream Shop tool (if relevant)

**No link shorteners.** Use Etsy direct or OpenMirror direct.

## Measuring Success (No Vanity Metrics)

Track only:
- **Clicks to Etsy** (Google Analytics / Etsy referrer)
- **Sales** (Etsy dashboard)
- **Repeat visitors** (who comes back)

Ignore:
- Likes (they don't predict sales)
- Comments (engagement ≠ revenue)
- Follower count (too early)

## Tools (Minimal)

- **Canva Pro:** Design posts, Reels, Stories
- **Buffer or Later:** Schedule posts 1 week ahead (optional, not required)
- **Etsy dashboard:** Track traffic source
- **Spreadsheet:** Track posting dates + which post drove which sales

## First Month Milestones

- [ ] Ship 5 products on Etsy (link in bio)
- [ ] Post 3x/week consistently for 4 weeks
- [ ] Hit 100 total clicks to shop
- [ ] Get 5 sales (any product)
- [ ] Collect 1-2 user stories
- [ ] Refine copy based on what resonates

## Next (After first month)

- Review what sold (rank by revenue, not vanity)
- Double down on top 2-3 products
- Ship next batch (6-10 new ideas)
- Consider TikTok if audience skews young (optional)
- Never add complexity until revenue justifies it

---

**Start simple. Ship first. Measure real outcomes. Repeat.**
